1.2 Interpretation
In this Deed, unless the context otherwise requires: (a) headings are for convenience only; (b) a reference
to a party includes that party's executors, administrators, and permitted assigns; and (c) the singular
includes the plural and vice versa.
2. OFFER
The Vendor has made a binding and definite offer to sell the Vessel to the Purchaser, as recorded in
Recital C above, and confirms that offer by way of this Deed.
3. ACCEPTANCE
The Purchaser hereby accepts the Vendor's offer in full, on the terms recorded in Recital D above,
including the inclusion of the trailer and outboard motor as part of the Vessel, and confirms that
acceptance by way of this Deed.
4. PURCHASE PRICE
The Purchase Price for the Vessel is $500.00 (five hundred dollars), payable by the Purchaser to the
Vendor at the Purchaser's discretion, as and when the Purchaser's personal financial circumstances allow,
following the Purchaser's part-time employment income becoming available. No fixed payment date or
payment schedule is specified.
5. INTENTION TO CREATE LEGAL RELATIONS
The parties intend this Deed to be legally binding and enforceable, and each party confirms that it is
entering into this Deed with the intention of creating formal legal relations, notwithstanding the informal and
family-based origins of the arrangement described in the Recitals.
