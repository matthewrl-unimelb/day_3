SUPPLY AGREEMENT
Between Arborvale Pty Ltd and Meridian Logistics Pty Ltd
RECITALS
A. The Supplier carries on the business of manufacturing and supplying commercial and office furniture
products throughout Australia.
B. The Purchaser carries on the business of providing logistics, warehousing and fit-out services to
corporate clients and requires a reliable ongoing supply of office furniture for on-sale and installation to its
own customers.
C. The Supplier has made an offer to supply, and the Purchaser has accepted that offer to purchase, the
Goods described in Schedule 1 on the terms and conditions set out in this Agreement.
D. The parties wish to record the full terms of their agreement in writing, and each confirms that it enters
into this Agreement with the clear and express intention of creating binding legal relations.
1. DEFINITIONS AND INTERPRETATION
1.1 Definitions
In this Agreement, unless the context otherwise requires, the following terms have the meanings set out
below.
“Agreement” means this Supply Agreement, including all Schedules, as amended from time to time in
accordance with clause 21.

for general banking business in Melbourne, Victoria.

“Confidential Information” means all information disclosed by one party to the other in connection with

confidential given its nature or the circumstances of disclosure.
