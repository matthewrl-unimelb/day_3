SCHEDULE 16 – PRIOR OFFERS AND NEGOTIATION HISTORY
No prior formal offers for the Vessel had been made or received by the Vendor before the correspondence
described in the Recitals. The Vendor had, on occasion in conversation with family members over the
preceding two years, mentioned that he “should probably sell the boat one day” given his declining ability
to use and maintain it, but no specific figure, timeframe, or prospective purchaser had previously been
discussed prior to the exchange of letters with the Purchaser described above.
