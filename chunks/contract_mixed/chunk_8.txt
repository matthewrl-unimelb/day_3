18. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
If a dispute arises between the parties, the parties must first attempt to resolve it through good faith
negotiation between senior representatives, and if not resolved within 21 days, either party may refer the
dispute to mediation before commencing court proceedings.
19. VARIATION
No variation of this Agreement is effective unless it is in writing and signed by authorised representatives
of both parties.
20. ASSIGNMENT
Neither party may assign or transfer any rights or obligations under this Agreement without the prior written
consent of the other party.
21. NOTICES
Any notice under this Agreement must be in writing and delivered by hand, prepaid post, or email to the
address of the recipient party set out in clause 2, and is deemed received in accordance with the usual
rules for each method of delivery.
22. GOVERNING LAW AND JURISDICTION
This Agreement is governed by the laws of Victoria, Australia, and the parties submit to the exclusive
