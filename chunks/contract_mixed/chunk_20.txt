SCHEDULE 31 – GENERAL NOTES ON CUSTOM JOINERY PROJECTS
(INFORMATION ONLY)
This Schedule reproduces general commentary of the kind commonly included in supply agreements of
this type, noting that custom joinery projects typically involve a design and consultation phase, a formal
quotation phase, a manufacturing phase, and an installation phase, and that clear agreement on payment
terms and any pre-contractual services (such as free design consultations) is generally considered good
practice to avoid later disputes.
