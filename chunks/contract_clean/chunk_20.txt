SCHEDULE 17 – REVIEW AND CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT
The Parties agree to conduct a joint performance review meeting no less than every 6 months during the
term of this Agreement, to discuss: (a) delivery performance against the milestones in Schedule 5; (b) any
recurring quality issues identified through the acceptance testing procedure in Schedule 8; (c)
opportunities for cost efficiencies or product improvements; and (d) forecast volumes for any anticipated
future orders. Minutes of each review meeting must be circulated to both Parties within 5 Business Days.
