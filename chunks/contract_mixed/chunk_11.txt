SCHEDULE 5 – GLOSSARY OF TRADE TERMS
“Joinery”: custom-built wooden or laminate fittings and furniture, manufactured off-site and installed to fit
a specific space. “Fit-out”: the process of preparing a commercial space for occupation, including
installation of fixtures, fittings, and furniture. “Variation Works”: additional work requested outside the
original agreed scope, typically charged at an hourly labour rate.
