SCHEDULE 6 – CARE AND MAINTENANCE INSTRUCTIONS
The Purchaser must ensure that end users of the Goods observe the following care instructions in order to
preserve the warranty in clause 12: (a) clean laminate surfaces only with a soft, damp cloth and mild
detergent; avoid abrasive cleaners or solvents; (b) do not exceed the rated load capacity of any desk or
chair; (c) periodically check and tighten fasteners on chair bases in accordance with the manufacturer's
user guide; (d) avoid exposing the Goods to direct sunlight or moisture for extended periods; and (e) report
any mechanical fault (e.g. gas lift failure, height adjustment malfunction) promptly to the Purchaser's
facilities team for assessment before continued use.
SCHEDULE 7 – COMPLIANCE CERTIFICATES
The Supplier must provide, prior to or at the time of delivery, the following documentation: (a) a Certificate
of Conformity confirming compliance with AS/NZS 4442:1997 and AS/NZS 4438:1997; (b) current FSC
Chain of Custody certification covering the timber components supplied under this Agreement; (c) a
product safety data sheet for any coatings or finishes used; and (d) a copy of the Supplier's current public
liability insurance certificate of currency.
