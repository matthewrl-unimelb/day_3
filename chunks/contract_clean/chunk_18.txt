SCHEDULE 14 – GLOSSARY OF INDUSTRY TERMS
NRC Rating: Noise Reduction Coefficient, a measure of the sound absorption performance of an acoustic
material, expressed as a value between 0 (no absorption) and 1 (full absorption).
Gas Lift Class: A safety classification (Class 1 to Class 4) applied to pneumatic height-adjustment
cylinders used in office seating, with higher classes indicating greater rated load capacity and safety
margin.
Chain of Custody Certification: Documentation verifying that timber or timber-derived materials have
been tracked from a certified sustainable source through each stage of processing, manufacturing, and
distribution.
Laminate: A composite surface material, typically comprising layers of resin-impregnated paper fused
under heat and pressure to a substrate, used for desk and table worktops due to its durability and
cost-effectiveness relative to solid timber or veneer.
