SCHEDULE 24 – GENERAL GLOSSARY OF CONTRACTUAL TERMS
“Counter-offer”: a response to an offer that introduces new or different terms, which operates as a
rejection of the original offer and a new offer in its own right. “Battle of the forms”: a situation arising
where each party seeks to contract on the basis of its own standard terms, and it is unclear whose terms, if
any, ultimately govern the transaction. “Past consideration”: something done or given before a promise
is made, which generally cannot serve as valid consideration for that later promise.
