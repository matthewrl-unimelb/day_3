DEED OF SALE OF VESSEL
Between Harold Kowalski (“Vendor”) and Ben Whitfield (“Purchaser”)
RECITALS
A. The Vendor, Harold Kowalski, is 79 years of age and resides at 22 Bayview Crescent, Frankston VIC
3199. The Vendor's wife of 52 years passed away approximately six months prior to the date of this Deed.
The Vendor has also experienced partial vision loss over the past 18 months, which he says makes
reading detailed paperwork difficult without assistance.
B. The Purchaser, Ben Whitfield, is the Vendor's great-nephew. Since the passing of the Vendor's wife, the
Purchaser has visited the Vendor regularly and has taken an increasingly active role in assisting the
Vendor with day-to-day paperwork, online banking, and correspondence. The Vendor has come to rely on
the Purchaser heavily for these matters and trusts him completely.

further to our chat last week, I might be willing to consider selling you the old Bertram someday, if the
timing ever felt right for me. No promises, but I wanted to put something in writing in case it helps you plan.
- Harold.”
