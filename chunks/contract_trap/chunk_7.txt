SCHEDULE 6 – GLOSSARY OF MARINE TERMS
Hull Identification Number (HIN): A unique 12-character identifier assigned to a vessel, analogous to a
vehicle identification number, used for registration and ownership tracking purposes.
Flybridge: An open control station situated above the main cabin of a cruiser, providing an elevated helm
position with additional visibility.
Rode: The line, chain, or combination of both connecting an anchor to a vessel.
Bilge Pump: A pump used to remove water that has accumulated in the lowest part of a vessel's hull.
