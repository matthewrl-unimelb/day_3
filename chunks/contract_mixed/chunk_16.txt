SCHEDULE 16 – QUALITY ASSURANCE PROCESS
Prior to delivery, the Supplier conducts an internal quality check of all manufactured joinery items against
the specifications in Schedule 1, including verification of dimensions, finish consistency, and hardware
function. Any item failing this internal check is remanufactured prior to delivery.
SCHEDULE 17 – SITE ACCESS AND WORKING HOURS
Installation work will be carried out between 7:00am and 4:00pm, Monday to Friday, to minimise disruption
to the Purchaser's business operations, which are expected to remain closed for renovation until the
Installation Date.
SCHEDULE 18 – WASTE AND PACKAGING REMOVAL
The Supplier is responsible for removing all packaging materials and installation waste from the
Purchaser's premises upon completion of installation, and for disposing of such waste in accordance with
applicable local council requirements.
SCHEDULE 19 – POST-INSTALLATION SUPPORT
Following installation, the Supplier will provide a single follow-up visit within 30 days to address any minor
adjustments (such as door alignment or hardware tightening) at no additional charge, provided such
adjustments do not constitute Variation Works as defined in clause 1.1.
