SCHEDULE 21 – GENERAL DEFINITIONS OF DEED TERMINOLOGY
(INFORMATION ONLY)
This Schedule is included for general reader assistance only, as is common in publicly available deed
templates, and explains commonly used terms. “Deed”: a type of binding legal document, historically
requiring a seal, now generally requiring signing, witnessing, and an indication of intention to be bound as
a deed. “Vendor”: the party selling the property or item the subject of the deed. “Purchaser”: the party
buying the property or item the subject of the deed. “Completion”: the point at which a transaction is
finalised, typically coinciding with payment and transfer of title.
