18. INSURANCE
The Purchaser must, from the Completion Date, arrange and maintain appropriate insurance cover for the
Vessel, including third-party liability cover, and must provide evidence of such cover to the Vendor upon
request. The Vendor makes no representation as to the insurability of the Vessel given its unregistered
status and incomplete service history as noted in Schedule 1.
19. MAINTENANCE OBLIGATIONS FOLLOWING TRANSFER
From the Completion Date, the Purchaser assumes sole responsibility for the maintenance, storage, and
safekeeping of the Vessel. The Vendor has no further obligation in respect of servicing, repair, or upkeep
of the Vessel following the transfer of possession recorded in Recital E.
20. INDEMNITY
The Purchaser indemnifies the Vendor against any loss, liability, cost, or claim arising from the Purchaser's
use, storage, operation, or ownership of the Vessel from the date possession transferred, including any
claim arising from the Vessel's unregistered status or any defect not disclosed in Schedule 1.
21. DISPUTE RESOLUTION
If any dispute arises between the parties in connection with this Deed, the parties must first attempt to
resolve the dispute through informal discussion between themselves before either party takes any further
action, including legal proceedings.
