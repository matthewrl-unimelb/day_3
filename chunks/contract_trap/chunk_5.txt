11. GOVERNING LAW
This Deed is governed by the laws of Victoria, Australia, and the parties submit to the exclusive jurisdiction
of the courts of Victoria.
12. NOTICES
Any notice under this Deed must be in writing and delivered by hand or prepaid post to the address of the
recipient party set out in the Recitals, or such other address as that party may notify in writing.
13. ENTIRE AGREEMENT
This Deed constitutes the entire agreement between the parties in relation to the sale of the Vessel and
supersedes all prior negotiations, representations, and agreements, whether written or oral, including the
correspondence referred to in the Recitals to the extent it is inconsistent with the terms of this Deed.
14. SEVERABILITY
If any provision of this Deed is held to be void, illegal, or unenforceable, that provision is severed from this
Deed and the remaining provisions continue in full force and effect.
15. FURTHER ASSURANCE
Each party must, at their own cost, do all things and execute all documents reasonably necessary to give
full effect to this Deed, including any documentation required to transfer registration of the trailer.
