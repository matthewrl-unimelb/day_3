22. ASSIGNMENT
Neither Party may assign, novate, or otherwise transfer any of its rights or obligations under this
Agreement without the prior written consent of the other Party, such consent not to be unreasonably
withheld.
23. NOTICES
Any notice given under this Agreement must be in writing and delivered by hand, prepaid post, or email to
the address of the recipient Party set out in clause 2 (or such other address as that Party may notify in
writing), and is deemed received: (a) if delivered by hand, at the time of delivery; (b) if sent by prepaid post,
3 Business Days after posting; and (c) if sent by email, at the time of transmission, provided no delivery
failure notification is received.

This Agreement is governed by the laws of Victoria, Australia. Each Party irrevocably submits to the
exclusive jurisdiction of the courts of Victoria and any courts entitled to hear appeals from those courts.

This Agreement, including its Schedules, constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties in relation
to its subject matter and supersedes all prior negotiations, representations, and agreements, whether
written or oral.
26. SEVERABILITY

from this Agreement and the remaining provisions continue in full force and effect.

all of which together constitute one and the same instrument.
