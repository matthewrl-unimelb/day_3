SCHEDULE 11 – BACKGROUND TO THE RELATIONSHIP BETWEEN THE
PARTIES
Solstice Interiors Pty Ltd and Bramwell & Co Joinery Pty Ltd were introduced through a mutual industry
contact in December 2025. The Purchaser was referred to the Supplier on the recommendation of a
nearby retailer who had used the Supplier's services for a similar fit-out project in 2024. Following an initial
phone conversation on 5 January 2026, the Supplier's design consultant arranged the free site visit
described in Recital C and Schedule 7.
Following the site visit, the parties exchanged several emails discussing possible finishes and budget
ranges before the Supplier prepared and issued the formal Offer described in Recital D. The verbal
discussion of the $185 per hour Variation Works rate, referred to in Recital F, took place during a phone
call on 15 January 2026 between the Supplier's project manager and the Purchaser's finance officer, Ms.
Priya Nair.
